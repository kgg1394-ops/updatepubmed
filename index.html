Mood and anxiety disorders are common in cancer survivors, and are associated with worse outcomes; however, systematic identification of high-risk patients remains limited, despite screening and intervention guidelines. A systematic review and meta-analysis were conducted to identify risk factors for mood and anxiety disorders, including diagnoses and clinically significant symptoms, in adult cancer survivors.

PubMed, Web of Science, Embase, PsycINFO, and Cochrane Central Register of Controlled Trials were searched for cohort and case-control studies published from January 2000 to November 2024 that evaluated cancer-related, sociodemographic, lifestyle, and psychosocial risk factors in adults with breast, lung, colorectal, prostate, stomach, liver, or pancreatic cancer. Fifty of the 53 eligible studies were included in the meta-analysis. Consistent risk factors included high tumor stage (vs. low; n = 28; RR, 1.55; 95% CI, 1.21-1.97), more physical comorbidities (vs. fewer; n = 36; RR, 1.57; 95% CI, 1.34-1.84), prior psychiatric history (high vs. low burden; n = 13; RR, 4.85; 95% CI, 2.77-8.50), female sex (vs. male; n = 19; RR, 1.52; 95% CI, 1.32-1.75), underweight body mass index (vs. normal; n = 6; RR, 1.93; 95% CI, 1.55-2.44), unmarried status (vs. married; n = 25; RR, 1.35; 95% CI, 1.14-1.58), and current smoking (vs. never; n = 12; RR, 1.27; 95% CI, 1.12-1.46). Suggestive associations, although less consistent in sensitivity analyses, were observed for high tumor grade, more extensive surgery, low income, low education, and low social support.

Several clinical and social factors were associated with increased risk of mood and anxiety outcomes in adult cancer survivors. These findings may support risk-based psychiatric screening and early intervention in resource-limited oncology settings.

Early urinary catheter removal after total mesorectal excision(TME) reduces urinary tract infections but increases the risk of acute urinary retention. Evidence on perioperative use of alpha-blockers in rectal cancer surgery remains limited. This study evaluated whether perioperative Alfuzosin or Silodosin reduces urinary re-catheterization after minimally invasive TME for rectal cancer.

A prospective multicentre cohort study(2021-2025) included male patients undergoing minimally invasive TME with low colorectal or coloanal anastomosis. Patients received either Alfuzosin (10 mg daily, day -3 to day +3), Silodosin (8 mg daily, day 0 to day +4), or no alpha-blocker (controls from the GRECCAR 10 trial). The primary endpoint was urinary re-catheterization within hospital stay. Secondary endpoints were catheterization duration, urinary and overall morbidity, and length of hospital stay.

Of 311 patients(Alfuzosin, n = 100; Silodosin, n = 100; controls, n = 111), urinary re-catheterization occurred in 4%, 8% and 14%, respectively. Alfuzosin significantly reduced the risk compared with controls (p = 0.016), whereas silodosin had no effect (p = 0.199). Urinary morbidity was lower in both alpha-blocker groups(9% and 8% vs. 18%). Median catheterization duration was 1, 2, and 4 days, and hospital stay was shorter with alpha-blockers (6 and 8 vs.10 days, p < 0.001). In multivariate analysis, Alfuzosin was associated with lower odds of urinary re-catheterization compared with controls (OR 0.27), while diabetes mellitus increased risk (OR 2.68).

Pembrolizumab plus lenvatinib is a standard first-line treatment for metastatic renal cell carcinoma (mRCC) based on the phase III CLEAR trial. Real-world data are needed to assess effectiveness and toxicity management in routine practice. We report a retrospective single-institution experience and a case of grade 4 immune-related pancreatitis followed by sustained tumour control after treatment discontinuation.

We retrospectively analysed consecutive patients with mRCC treated with first-line pembrolizumab plus lenvatinib at IRCCS Regina Elena from March 2024 through March 2026, with clinical follow-up updated through September 2026. The primary endpoint was objective response rate (ORR). Survival was estimated by the Kaplan-Meier method.

Thirty-eight patients were treated, and all were evaluable for response. ORR was 78.9% (4 complete responses and 26 partial responses). 💡 Conclusion
Pancreatic cancer is one of the hardest cancers to treat. Most patients receive standard chemotherapy that has improved only modestly in two decades, partly because the tumour is wrapped in dense scar-like tissue and carries a fault in a gene called KRAS that was long thought impossible to target with drugs. Researchers can now grow tiny three-dimensional copies of a patient’s own tumour in the laboratory. These living models, called organoids, let many drugs be tested on a patient’s cancer before a treatment is chosen. This review explains how well that approach works today. I focus on a landmark clinical trial reported in 2025, the first to test organoid-guided treatment in a randomised study. Overall survival did not improve. Most patients who went on to a second treatment never received an organoid-matched drug, most often because they became too unwell during the weeks needed to grow the models and run the tests. Yet the small group who did receive a treatment matched to their organoid lived markedly longer. This early signal is promising but not yet proven, and the process is currently too slow for many patients. I also describe two ways the field is advancing: growing organoids together with the surrounding scar tissue so testing better reflects the real tumour, and combining new KRAS-blocking drugs to overcome resistance. The practical message is clear. To help patients, researchers must shorten the time from biopsy to treatment and design smarter drug-testing panels, so that a biologically promising tool finally delivers a survival benefit in the clinic.

▶Low-glycemic load diet enhances pancreatic β-cell function through the fucose-Rnf183 axis in type 2 diabetes mellitus.

📌 Order: 📌 환자 맞춤 판단
💡 Conclusion
Low-GL diet reshapes the serum metabolome of T2DM patients and increases fucose abundance. Fucose enhances pancreatic β-cell function, partly through suppression of ubiquitination and Rnf183. These findings provide mechanistic insights into dietary interventions for diabetes management.
Our previous studies demonstrated that low-glycemic load (GL) diets reduced diabetes risk and promoted remission in type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM), but the underlying mechanisms remain unclear.

Serum samples from newly diagnosed T2DM patients with random 3-month low-GL dietary intervention or conventional energy-restricted diet were analyzed using LC-MS-based metabolomics. High glucose (HG)-treated MIN6 cells with or without fucose were used to assess β-cell function, apoptosis, and transcriptomic sequencing. Rnf183 was knocked down in MIN6 cells.

Metabolomic analysis revealed that metabolites identified by LC-MS were mostly classified as carboxylic acids and derivatives, fatty acyls, and glycerophospholipids. Fucose exhibited the highest VIP score among all differential metabolites and was significantly upregulated in T2DM patients with a low-GL diet compared to those without a low-GL diet. KEGG enrichment analysis indicated that fucose-associated fructose and mannose metabolism was affected by the low-GL diet. Fucose enhanced the expression of key β-cell functional genes (Pdx1, Nkx6.1, Mafa, and Insulin2) and attenuated HG-induced apoptosis in MIN6 cells. Transcriptomic analysis revealed that fucose regulated T2DM progression-related pathways (Notch signaling and insulin secretion) and protein ubiquitination in HG-induced MIN6 cells. Correspondingly, fucose reduced global ubiquitination of MIN6 cells and downregulated ubiquitination regulator Rnf183. Rnf183 knockdown increased expression levels of key β-cell functional genes in HG-treated MIN6 cells.

Low-GL diet reshapes the serum metabolome of T2DM patients and increases fucose abundance. Fucose enhances pancreatic β-cell function, partly through suppression of ubiquitination and Rnf183. These findings provide mechanistic insights into dietary interventions for diabetes management.
